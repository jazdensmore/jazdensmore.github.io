About

I'm a GTM Revenue Operations specialist and Certified Salesforce Administrator with over 15 years building and operating the technical infrastructure behind high-performing revenue teams. 07 Flagship
Self-Serve Duplicate Account Review Engine
The most complex tool in the suite. An internally hosted AI-powered application that enabled Sales and RevOps to perform duplicate account reviews without routing to Data Ops. Built on institutional knowledge that had never been formally documented, it featured a rigorous decision matrix covering 9 distinct scenarios - each documented in Mermaid charts - with branching logic to determine whether accounts should be merged, data updated, or hierarchies built. When a merge was appropriate, the tool surfaced the recommended "winner" account with AI-generated rationale. Queries that had been run manually for every individual review were fully automated. The tool reached testing phase before the role was eliminated.
